# Card

A surface that groups related content and separates it from what surrounds
it. Cards carry the elevated surface color and a border.

## When to use

Use a card when a group of content is a unit the user will act on or
compare against its peers: a product, an order, a review. A list of cards
reads as a set of comparable things.

Do not use a card merely to put a border around something. Where the goal
is separation rather than grouping, spacing and a heading do the job
without adding a visual container.

## Nesting

Cards do not nest. Two borders inside one another produce ambiguous
grouping and the inner card's elevation fights the outer one. Content
inside a card is separated with spacing and headings.

## Content

A card usually leads with an image or a heading and ends with an action.
Where a card has an action, it is one action. A card carrying three
competing actions is a layout that needs to be split.
